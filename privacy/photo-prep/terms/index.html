JPEG는 손실 압축 형식이므로 글자·색·세부 표현과 가독성이 달라질 수 있습니다. 투명 PNG는 사용자가 흰 배경 변환을 명시적으로 허용해야 JPEG로 바꿉니다. 원본 메타데이터가 결과에 복사되지 않으며, 파일을 받는 서비스의 변환·표시는 앱이 통제하지 않습니다.

Supported inputs and checking output

The app processes one supported JPEG or static 8-bit PNG. Inputs are limited to 12 MiB, 12 million pixels and 8,192 pixels on either edge; unsupported or corrupt files are rejected. Output is JPEG with a separate 8 MiB hard ceiling. Optional file-size limits range from 10 KB to 2,000 KB; 1 KB means 1,000 bytes and 1 MiB means 1,048,576 bytes. Also check the limits displayed in the app.

JPEG is lossy: text, color, detail and readability may change. A transparent PNG requires your explicit approval for a white background before conversion. Source metadata is not copied into the result. Photo Prep cannot control conversions or display by a receiving service.

Pixel bounds preserve aspect ratio and do not upscale. Further pixel reduction to meet a file-size limit requires separate permission. An unmet limit is not presented as successful. Before saving or sharing, inspect the actual result for coverage, readability, pixel dimensions and file size.

Manual masking does not automatically discover private information or guarantee that every private detail is hidden or mosaic content is unrecoverable. Check sensitive material before sharing it.
