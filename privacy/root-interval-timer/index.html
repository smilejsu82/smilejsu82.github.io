Who provides the app

ROOT GAMES (ROOTGAMES) provides Root Interval Timer, a configurable work/rest timer. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to smilejsu82@gmail.com, with “Root Interval Timer” in the subject. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Your timer information

The app keeps timer durations, rounds, language, sound/vibration choices, notification-prompt state, session progress and the latest20 completed-session records in app-owned storage on your device. Completion records include a session identifier, timer settings, completion time and active elapsed duration. These records support timer operation, recovery and your local history. The app does not measure heart rate or other body metrics, use Health Connect, or provide an app account or cloud-sync service. The current app does not upload your saved timer routines or completion history to our own server or use those records for ad targeting.

Storage and deletion

Settings and session data have no automatic day-based expiry. When the20-record history limit is exceeded, older entries are replaced. When no session/service is active, Clear completed history clears completed records and the saved session snapshot; your timer preferences remain. Clearing the app's storage in Android or uninstalling normally removes its app-owned data. Device backup is disabled in the identified current build. These operations are not a guarantee of secure forensic erasure or control over every device's transfer/backup features. We cannot remotely read or delete the local records on your phone.

Google libraries and this version

This version does not show production ads or enable app-level usage analytics. Google libraries are included and may perform technical initialization; disabled collection settings do not mean that no library can initialize or communicate. Technical SDK processing may involve IP addresses, app/device identifiers and diagnostics, depending on the functions and settings in use. See Google's privacy information and information about partner apps. A future version that enables advertising or measurement will update this notice and the relevant choices before activation.

Questions, rights and correspondence

You may contact the address above about access, correction, deletion, restrictions, objections or withdrawing a relevant consent, as applicable to your information and local law. Local history can be cleared on your device as described above; deleting local records does not delete messages you voluntarily email us. Email enquiries are handled through the email service and the operator. Proposed operating rule, owner confirmation required: we keep correspondence while necessary to respond and resolve the matter, then delete information no longer needed; records needed for a legal obligation or dispute are retained only for that purpose while necessary. Applicable rights may have limits, and requests may require proportionate identity verification. You can also complain to the privacy authority in your jurisdiction, including the UK ICO where applicable.

This policy website and changes

If this page is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes visitor IP addresses for service security under its privacy statement. The proposed static policy page contains no ROOTGAMES analytics, advertising or enquiry form. This concerns website visits, not an upload of your timer history. We will identify material changes to this policy and its effective date on this page.
