Fruit Fly Idle / 초파리 키우기 Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

ROOTGAMES provides Fruit Fly Idle as an advertising-supported game. The game does not require an account and does not operate its own game server.

Data processed

Game progress and settings are stored locally on the device. Google Firebase Analytics processes app interactions, approximate usage statistics, device/app identifiers and diagnostics so we can understand stability and improve the game. Google AdMob processes advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers to deliver, measure and prevent fraud in advertising.

Advertising and choices

The game shows a banner and optional rewarded video ads. Rewarded ads are shown only after the player chooses a clearly labelled “Watch Ad” action. Where required, Google’s consent form is shown before ads are requested. Advertising privacy choices can be reopened from the in-game Settings screen.

Sharing and retention

Data may be processed by Google as our analytics and advertising provider under its own policies. We do not sell personal information or operate an independent user database. Local game progress remains until the app data or app is deleted. Provider retention is governed by Google’s policies and our provider settings.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

You may decline optional rewarded ads, change available advertising privacy choices in Settings, reset local data by clearing app storage, or uninstall the game. Requests about this policy may be sent to smilejsu82@gmail.com.
